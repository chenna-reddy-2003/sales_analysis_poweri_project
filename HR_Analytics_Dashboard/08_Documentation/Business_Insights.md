# HR ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
## BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT

### 1. Introduction

The HR Analytics Dashboard is developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data and understand workforce trends.

This report explains the business insights that HR teams can explore using employee information and dashboard visualizations.

### 2. Employee Overview

The Employee Count KPI provides an overview of the total employees in the organization.

**Business Insight:**
- Helps HR understand the workforce size.
- Supports employee planning.
- Provides a basic view of organizational strength.

### 3. Employee Attrition Analysis

The Attrition KPI and Attrition Rate KPI help HR understand employee exits.

**Business Insight:**
- Monitor employee attrition.
- Identify workforce groups that may need attention.
- Support employee retention planning.

### 4. Attrition by Age Group

The age-wise attrition chart helps compare employee exits across different age groups.

**Business Insight:**
- Identify age groups with comparatively higher attrition.
- Understand workforce age-related patterns.
- Plan suitable employee engagement activities.

### 5. Attrition by Gender

The gender-based visualization compares attrition across gender categories.

**Business Insight:**
- Understand gender-wise attrition patterns.
- Identify differences that require further investigation.
- Support inclusive HR planning.

### 6. Attrition by Education

The Education Field donut chart helps analyze attrition across employee education backgrounds.

**Business Insight:**
- Compare attrition across education categories.
- Understand workforce education patterns.
- Support recruitment and retention analysis.

### 7. Attrition by Job Role

The Job Role chart helps HR compare employee attrition across different job positions.

**Business Insight:**
- Identify job roles with comparatively higher attrition.
- Understand role-specific workforce challenges.
- Support role-based retention strategies.

### 8. Attrition by Salary Slab

The salary-based chart helps compare attrition across salary categories.

**Business Insight:**
- Understand attrition patterns across salary bands.
- Identify salary groups that need further analysis.
- Support compensation review discussions.

### 9. Years at Company Analysis

The Years at Company visualization helps explore the relationship between employee tenure and attrition.

**Business Insight:**
- Understand employee experience and tenure patterns.
- Identify tenure groups with higher employee exits.
- Support employee engagement and retention planning.

### 10. Job Satisfaction Analysis

The Job Role and Job Satisfaction matrix helps compare satisfaction levels across different job roles.

**Business Insight:**
- Understand job satisfaction patterns.
- Identify roles that may need employee feedback.
- Support workplace improvement initiatives.

### 11. Department-wise Analysis

The Department slicer allows users to filter the dashboard by department.

**Business Insight:**
- Compare workforce metrics across departments.
- Analyze department-level attrition.
- Support department-specific HR decisions.

### 12. Recommendations

Based on the patterns identified in the dashboard, HR teams can consider:

1. Conduct employee feedback surveys.
2. Review employee retention strategies.
3. Monitor job satisfaction.
4. Review compensation policies where appropriate.
5. Improve employee engagement programs.
6. Investigate departments or roles with higher attrition.

### 13. Overall Business Benefits

- Better workforce monitoring.
- Improved HR reporting.
- Easier identification of attrition patterns.
- Better employee retention planning.
- Data-driven decision-making.

### 14. Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides useful insights into employee attrition, salary, job satisfaction, age groups, education and job roles.

By using Microsoft Power BI visualizations and DAX measures, HR teams can explore workforce patterns and make informed decisions.

**Note:** These are analysis areas and recommendations, not confirmed findings. Actual business insights should be written after reviewing the KPI values and charts in the Power BI dashboard.
