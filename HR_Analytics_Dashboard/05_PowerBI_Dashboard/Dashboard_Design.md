# HR Analytics Dashboard – Design Documentation

## 1. Project Title
HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## 2. Dashboard Objective
The main objective of this dashboard is to analyze employee data, monitor employee attrition, understand workforce trends, and support HR decision-making.

## 3. Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query Editor
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power BI Visualizations

## 4. Data Source
Dataset Name: HR_Analytics.csv

Main Table: HR_Analytics Data

Important Columns:
- EmpID
- Age
- Gender
- Department
- JobRole
- EducationField
- MonthlyIncome
- YearsAtCompany
- Attrition_Count
- JobSatisfaction
- SalarySlab
- AgeGroup

## 5. Dashboard KPI Cards

The dashboard contains the following KPI cards:

1. Employee Count – Total number of employees.
2. Attrition – Number of employees who left the company.
3. Attrition Rate – Percentage of employees who left.
4. Average Age – Average age of employees.
5. Average Salary – Average monthly salary.
6. Average Years – Average years employees worked at the company.

## 6. Dashboard Visualizations

### 6.1 Donut Chart
Title: Attrition by Education

Purpose:
Shows employee attrition based on education field.

### 6.2 Column Chart
Title: Attrition by Age

Purpose:
Analyzes attrition across different age groups.

### 6.3 Bar Chart
Title: Attrition by Job Role

Purpose:
Identifies job roles with higher employee attrition.

### 6.4 Bar Chart
Title: Attrition by Salary

Purpose:
Analyzes attrition across different salary slabs.

### 6.5 Area Chart
Title: Attrition by Years at Company

Purpose:
Shows employee attrition based on years of experience in the company.

### 6.6 Treemap
Title: Attrition by Gender

Purpose:
Compares employee attrition across gender categories.

### 6.7 Matrix
Title: Job Role and Job Satisfaction

Purpose:
Displays job satisfaction across different job roles.

## 7. Dashboard Slicer

Department Slicer:
Allows HR users to filter dashboard information by department.

## 8. Dashboard Design Principles

- Use a clean and professional layout.
- Keep KPI cards at the top.
- Use suitable chart titles.
- Maintain consistent fonts and colors.
- Avoid unnecessary visual elements.
- Make the dashboard easy to understand.
- Ensure slicers interact correctly with visuals.

## 9. Business Benefits

- Helps identify employee attrition trends.
- Supports workforce planning.
- Helps HR understand employee satisfaction.
- Identifies departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Supports data-driven HR decisions.

## 10. Expected Outcome

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard that helps HR teams monitor employee information, analyze attrition, and understand workforce trends.

## 11. Project Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can be used to transform HR data into meaningful visual insights. The dashboard helps organizations understand employee attrition and make better HR decisions.
